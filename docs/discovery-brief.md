## 8. Implications for future site / marketing

### Recommendation
Treat **Legacy Realty LLC as the flagship brokerage brand** (HI compliance, MLS, hiring, PB authority). Treat the **Nevada practice as a market arm / co-branded personal brand** that feeds the same dual-market story — not a second unrelated business.

### Practical architecture options (decide after interview)
1. **Hub + spokes:** One Legacy-led site with HI MLS + NV market pages + relocation funnel; retire or 301 `homesbylizak.com`.  
2. **Two live sites:** Keep HI MLS platform; rebuild NV site with synced branding and clear “also Legacy PB / dual licensed” disclosure.  
3. **Interim:** Fix/redirect dead NV domain ASAP so Google/social traffic is not wasted, then unify brand.
