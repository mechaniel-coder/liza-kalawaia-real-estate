Real estate for me has never been just about buying or selling property — it’s about helping people build stability, opportunity, and long-term legacy for their families. I began my real estate career in Nevada in 2018 and quickly built my business through hard work, honesty, communication, and genuine care for my clients. Over the years, I became known for being hands-on, straightforward, and deeply committed to protecting my clients throughout every step of the transaction. Those same values became the foundation of Legacy Realty LLC. Born and raised in Hawaii, opening my own brokerage was more than a business move — it was personal. Legacy Realty was built on the belief that clients deserve real guidance, strong representation, and a brokerage that treats people like family, not just another transaction. Whether helping first-time buyers, local families, military clients, investors, or those relocating between Hawaii and the mainland, my goal is always the same: make the process smooth, transparent, and successful. As a Principal Broker, I believe professionalism and compliance matter. Hawaii real estate laws place strong responsibility on brokers to properly supervise transactions, maintain ethical standards, and protect the public. That level of responsibility is something I take seriously every day in how Legacy Realty operates and serves its clients. Beyond real estate, family is my biggest motivation. I understand the importance of home, security, and creating something meaningful for future generations. That mindset carries into every client relationship and every business decision I make. At Legacy Realty LLC, we’re not here to be the biggest brokerage — we’re here to make a lasting impact through integrity, hard work, and service that people remember. Your Legacy Starts Here.
