About Liza Kalawaia Real Estate

Liza Kalawaia - Your Trusted Realtor in Pahrump and Las Vegas Since 2018

With over six years of experience in the real estate market, Liza Kalawaia is a dedicated and results-driven agent serving Pahrump and Las Vegas. Since 2018, she has been passionate about helping clients achieve their real estate goals, whether they are buying their dream home, selling a property, or investing in the dynamic Nevada market.

Liza takes pride in her personalized approach, ensuring every client receives the attention and guidance they deserve throughout the entire transaction process. Her extensive market knowledge, strong negotiation skills, and commitment to excellence have earned her a reputation as a trusted advocate in the community.

Having set an ambitious goal to close 65 transactions and achieve over $10 million in sales revenue annually, Liza’s unwavering dedication to her craft speaks for itself. Whether you’re a first-time buyer, seasoned investor, or ready to sell, Liza is here to guide you with integrity, professionalism, and a keen understanding of the market.

When you choose Liza Kalawaia as your real estate partner, you’re not just getting an agent – you’re gaining a trusted ally who is committed to turning your real estate dreams into reality.
